Electrical gadgets

Electronic items are devices that use electric current to process, control, or transmit information, typically relying on semiconductor components like transistors and integrated circuits (microchips). They range from complex computers to simple household appliances and are integral to modern life.

Vegetables

Vegetables are any edible part of a plant, including leaves, stems, roots, tubers, flowers, and even certain fruits like tomatoes and cucumbers, that are typically consumed as part of a meal. They are distinct from culinary fruits, nuts, and grains, and are valued for their nutritional content, such as vitamins, minerals, and fiber.
